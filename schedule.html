🎯 Multi-Select Filtering

Tip: Select multiple conferences and teams to see games matching ANY of your selections (OR logic).

Conference Filtering
  • Check multiple conferences to see games from any of them
  • Use "Power 5 Only" for quick ACC/Big Ten/Big 12/Pac-12/SEC filtering
  • "Select All" or "Clear" for quick reset
Team Filtering
  • Check multiple teams to see their games
  • Use the search box to quickly find specific teams
  • Selection summary shows how many teams are active

💡 Power User Tips

⭐ Favorites: Click stars on pitchers, teams, or games to save favorites. They sync across devices via Supabase!
📊 CSV Export: Export filtered game data with all pitcher details for analysis in Excel/Google Sheets.
🔍 Search: Search by team name or pitcher name. Works across all games and rosters instantly.
📅 Week Selection: Select multiple weeks to see games across your chosen date range.
🎯 Priority Levels: Set game priorities and sort by them to focus on must-watch matchups.
