💡 Power User Tips

⭐ Favorites: Click stars next to pitcher names to save favorites. They sync across devices via Supabase!
📊 CSV Export: Export filtered roster data with all pitcher details for analysis in Excel/Google Sheets.
🔍 Quick Navigation: Use the navigation links at the top to switch between Rosters, Schedule, and Analytics.
📈 Pitcher Details: Click pitcher headshots to zoom in and see details. Position badges show LHP/RHP/P classifications.
⚡ Performance: Images load lazily for smooth scrolling. Data is cached for 24 hours for instant loading.
